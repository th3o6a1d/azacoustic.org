# Hiring Musicians & Events

AZ Acoustic Collective is always looking to connect with talented musicians and organize events throughout Phoenix and the surrounding areas.

## For Musicians

Are you a musician specializing in bluegrass, folk, or old time music? We're interested in:

- **Featured Performers**: Musicians who can lead workshops or perform at our events
- **Session Musicians**: Skilled players for recording projects or special performances
- **Instructors**: Experienced teachers to lead workshops and classes

## For Event Organizers

We provide:

- **Live Music**: Acoustic ensembles for private events, festivals, and gatherings
- **Workshops**: Educational sessions on bluegrass, folk, and old time music
- **Custom Performances**: Tailored musical experiences for your event

## Get in Touch

Interested in working with us? Please fill out the contact form below with:

- Your name and contact information
- Your musical background and experience
- The type of opportunity you're interested in
- Any relevant links (website, recordings, videos)
